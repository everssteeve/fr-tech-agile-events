# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Practitioners of the French tech and agile scene: agile coaches, Scrum Masters, product managers, developers, tech leads, engineering managers, conference organisers and speakers. They open the site in two moments:

- **Weekly check-in** (desktop, office or home, a few minutes): "what is happening this week and next, what did I miss last week?"
- **Deep read** (desktop or phone, commute or evening, 10 to 20 minutes): read the synthesis of a conference they attended or could not attend, jump to a session, watch its video, share a link with their team.

Many are speakers themselves and come back to see what was said about a given edition.

## Product Purpose

A living calendar of French tech and agile conferences with, for every past edition, a synthesis of what participants retained (session by session, then the cross-cutting ideas), linked videos, and a monthly "synthesis of syntheses". Content is refreshed every night. Success: a practitioner finds this week's events in one glance and understands what an edition was about in two minutes, with a path to go deeper.

## Brand Personality

Informed, sober, generous. The tone of a well-read colleague who went to every conference and takes careful notes: factual, attributed, honest about gaps. Never marketing, never hype.

## Anti-references

- Event-listing marketplaces (Eventbrite, Meetup): ticket-first, noisy cards, promotional banners.
- SaaS dashboards with hero metrics and gradient accents.
- News portals with infinite feeds and no sense of place in time.
- AI-summary sites that hide sources and uncertainty.

## Design Principles

1. **Time is the interface.** Every view answers "where am I in time?" (this week, this month, this year) and lets the reader move to past or future in one gesture.
2. **Always a way out.** Navigation, search and "back to now" are reachable from anywhere without scrolling through content.
3. **Reading first.** Syntheses are long-form: comfortable measure, clear hierarchy, anchors, and the next step (video, source, related month) at hand.
4. **Show the uncertainty.** Date confidence, thin corpus and missing feedback are visible, never hidden.
5. **Earned familiarity.** Standard, predictable patterns over novelty; the content is the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard navigation for every control, visible focus, prefers-reduced-motion respected, color never the only carrier of meaning (category chips carry text labels), light and dark themes, readable on a 360 px phone.
